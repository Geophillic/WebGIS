About Me

My name is Oladipupo Ademola Ibrahim, and I am currently pursuing an MSc in GIS and Spatial Planning at the University of Porto, Portugal. I hold an MS in Disaster Science and Climate Resilience from the University of Dhaka, Bangladesh, and a B.Tech in Remote Sensing and GIS from the Federal University of Technology Akure, Nigeria.

My technical capabilities include spatial analysis, remote sensing and cartography, with practical experience using ArcGIS Pro, including for wildfire susceptibility modelling. I am also developing my skills in HTML, CSS and JavaScript to build interactive web maps and communicate geospatial information online. I value analytical thinking, attention to detail, clear communication and collaborative problem-solving. I am looking to contribute these capabilities in an industry setting while continuing to develop practical skills in geospatial analysis and web mapping.

My professional interests lie in applying geospatial technologies to environmental assessment, land-use planning and urban resilience. I am particularly interested in turning spatial data into useful information that helps organisations understand environmental challenges, assess vulnerability and make informed planning decisions.
